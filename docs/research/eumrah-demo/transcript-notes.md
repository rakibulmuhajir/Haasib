# Cleaned transcript notes

This is a normalized research summary, not a verbatim transcript.

## Dashboard and drill-downs

- Headline counts: tickets and refunds, visa approvals, hotel-only passengers, Umrah vouchers and voucher passengers, tour vouchers.
- System summary: receivables, payables, cash and bank, visa status, Umrah/tour/ticket booking counts and amounts, other-service invoices.
- Client balance summary shows the largest receivables and advances.
- Visa approval and voucher summary is broken down by B2B agent/client: visas, hotel-only passengers, vouchers, voucher passengers, and passengers still missing a voucher.
- Summary values drill into passenger-level lists with group, passport, approval date, passenger name/type, Saudi company, MOFA number, visa status, and voucher reference.

## Voucher control

- Pending voucher list includes voucher/reference, client, date, departure, head passenger, package, owner/office, and actions.
- A complete voucher can be approved; an incomplete one remains in a partial-edit state.
- Vouchers can be filtered by client and date and opened from the list.
- The printed voucher can be branded for the company or agent and includes a QR code.

## Voucher contents

- Passenger names, passports, dates of birth, visa numbers/dates, Saudi company, and voucher status.
- Outbound and return flight number, origin/destination, departure/arrival date and time.
- Any number of hotel stays with city, check-in/out, nights, room type/count, meal plan, view, hotel confirmation, and potentially room number.
- Transport can be entered as a complete package or by sector, such as Jeddah–Makkah, Makkah–Madinah, Madinah–Makkah, and Makkah–Jeddah.
- Transport rows include vehicle, shared/private type, quantity, date, pickup time, related flight/bus reference, pickup point, destination, and booking reference.
- Ziyarat rows include city/tour, vehicle, sharing/private type, passenger count, date, pickup time, and pickup point.
- Terms can be English or Urdu.
- Contact details can be fixed or selected dynamically from the hotels, transport, and ziyarat providers present in the voucher.

## Operational and hotel reports

- Hotel-wise reports can be filtered by period, city, client, hotel, and supplier.
- Hotel output includes guest, city, check-in/out, nights, hotel, room type, room/bed totals, supplier, reference, and payable.
- A daily KSA status panel shows arrivals, departures, Makkah check-ins/outs, Madinah check-ins/outs, current planned population in each city, and pilgrims recorded as having exited Saudi Arabia.
- Arrival reports can be filtered by period, client, arrival airport, Saudi company, transport provider, and flight.
- The demonstrated arrival report includes voucher/reference, client, passport, head passenger, pax, route, arrival date, flight, airport, arrival time, hotel, room type, checkout date, transport type/provider, and Saudi company.
- Hotel check-in reports distinguish whether the party is coming from Pakistan, Makkah, or Madinah.
- The reports menu includes arrival, departure, check-in, check-out, inter-city trip, visa-only, and hotel passenger/night summary reports.

## Other areas shown

- Umrah quotations, tour bookings, ticket bookings, hotel reservation status, Umrah booking register, pending queues, and cancelled bookings.
- Ticket purchase/sale, refunds, airline arrival/departure reports, sales invoices, and tax reports.
- General vouchers, cash/bank receipts and payments, receivable/payable summaries, trial balance, balance sheet, standard/head-wise ledgers, invoice-wise statements, aging, partial allocations, and unadjusted payments.
- Group ticket purchase and sale with PNR inventory showing sold and remaining seats.
- Bulk MOFA import of passport/visa details and manual entry.
- Admin, staff, agent, and Makkah/Madinah staff profiles with configurable rights.
