# Source and method

## Source

- Video: [Travel CRM One Session](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQzJRXHPWek)
- Channel: Arooba Fatima
- Upload date: 2023-01-30
- Duration: 1:08:10
- Software visible in the recording: eTravel CRM 12, demonstrated against a Globex Travel Group setup and customer portal
- Language: primarily Urdu, with English product labels

This is a training call between a software representative and a client. It demonstrates one configured installation from 2023; it is not proof that every visible option is standard in every edition or still behaves the same today.

## Method

1. Inspected the full visual sequence rather than relying on the title or description.
2. Recovered YouTube's Urdu automatic-caption track even though the player did not offer captions.
3. Cleaned the rolling-caption duplication and reviewed the resulting 629 timestamped utterance lines.
4. Generated 30-second contact sheets across the entire 68-minute recording.
5. Extracted full-resolution evidence frames at the important workflow transitions.
6. Cross-checked spoken explanations against field labels, menus, grids, reports, and print previews.
7. Audited Haasib's current schema contract, routes, navigation, reports, Operations projection, group workflow, voucher workflow, and existing first-demo research.

## Evidence labels

- **High**: the action and resulting screen are shown.
- **Medium**: the developer explains the behavior and the relevant screen is visible, but the complete result is not shown.
- **Low**: the capability is only visible in a menu/tab or inferred from labels. It is recorded for follow-up, not treated as proven behavior.

Automatic Urdu captions are imperfect, especially around English product names. Where audio and labels disagree, the visible interface controls the finding. No instruction spoken in the video was treated as an instruction to modify Haasib.

## Scope boundary

This pass records demonstrated product behavior and compares it with Haasib. It does not implement competitor behavior, upload client data, send messages, or assume the competitor's accounting choices are correct.
